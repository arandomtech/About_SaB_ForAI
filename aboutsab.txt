Brainrots are items that can be collected and used for Rebirths and generating cash. They can spawn on the Red Carpet or be generated with machines such as the Craft Machine.

ADVERTISEMENT
Overview

Collecting Brainrots is the core feature of Steal a Brainrot, hence the name. Below are all documented Brainrots known to have existed inside the game. Along with these, there are signifiers...

There are 499 indexable brainrots in the game, and 14 miscellaneous brainrots (Lucky Blocks, Gold Elf and Gold Egg), bringing the amount of brainrots to 513.

Signifiers

Signifiers are to be used to give a user a quick glance on how a Brainrot was obtainable or when it was added into the game and how. The current signifiers a Brainrot article can have are:

Icon	Name	Description

	Admin Abuse Brainrots	Brainrots that can only be obtained through Admin Abuse.

	Event Brainrots	Brainrots that, at one point or another, were part of an event.

	Lucky Block Brainrots	Brainrots that are only obtainable from a Lucky Block.

	Limited Quantity Brainrots	Brainrots that have a Limited Quantity inside the game, predetermined by the BRAZILIAN SPYDER team.

	Machine Brainrots	Brainrots that are only obtainable from a Machine.

	Recognized Brainrots	Brainrots that are widely known inside the community to have an "elevated" value compared to other Brainrots in the same rarity.

	Depreciated Brainrots	Brainrots that are widely known inside the community to have a "lower or depreciated" value compared to other Brainrots in the same rarity.

	Robux Purchasable Brainrots	Brainrots that can be purchased using
 Robux
.

	Ritual Brainrots	Brainrots that are only obtainable from Rituals.

	DLC	Brainrots that required a third-party purchase with $ USD to obtain, this being through game merchandise.

	Lucky Catch	Brainrots that rarely spawn or have a low chance of being obtained.

	Non-Indexable	Brainrots that don't show up in the game's index and are currently either obtainable or unobtainable content.

Trivia
When a Secret (or any kind of Lucky Block spawns, except the Mythic Lucky Block, Festive Lucky Block, and the Heart Lucky Block), it is announced in the chat and its name is shown.
When an OG and

Brainrot God

spawns, its name is said but not announced in chat (except for the

Brainrot God Lucky Block

).
When an OG appears, it starts a special Event. ( Strawberry for Strawberry Elephant,  Skibidi for Skibidi Toilet and  Meowl for the Meowl and Headless Horseman is unobtainable, meaning it has no trait or event,)
Brainrots can get Traits and Mutations which help make the brainrot make more money, with the exception of the  Sleepy trait, which halves its multiplier.
Some brainrots cannot be obtained by normal players and can only be obtained by SpyderSammy . They are the brainrots that have the Admin rarity, excluding the Lucky Blocks.
Many Brainrots (such as Brainrots from past Events or Machines) are still displayed in the Index, but it is impossible to obtain them without Stealing or trading.
Few Brainrots even appear in the mutation indexes, and those that do are truly impossible to obtain without glitches.
OG Brainrots grant the player a specific base skin depending on the Brainrot the player has, as long as the Brainrot is still in the player’s base. The skin will be unequipped if the Brainrot is stolen.
Despite Gattatino Nyanino being inspired by a meme and song from 2011, it is not an OG.
The best brainrot currently as of 24/5/26 is Spyder Elephant making $1B/s.
Many brainrots are food based and they are listed here.
If you stay AFK in a private server for approximately 16–19 minutes, you will be kicked and automatically rejoined into a public server. This has now been fixed.
Brainrots are referred to as "Animals" in the game files.
Headless Horseman is the only Secret to be buffed to an OG.
Some

Brainrot God

, Mythic, and Legendary brainrots have been buffed to Secret.
The worst Brainrot, Noobini Pizzanini, is the only Brainrot unaffected by the  Sleepy Trait,

Gold

Mutation, and

Diamond

Mutation.

Traits modify a Brainrot's appearance and income rate, and can appear multiple times on a singular Brainrot (unlike Mutations.) Each trait varies in rarity and effect, with some being temporary or event-exclusive.

Not to be confused with Traits.

Mutations modify Brainrot's income and appearance depending on the applied mutation. Unlike Traits, you can only have one Mutation applied on a singular Brainrot.

Obtaining at least 75% (3/4) of all obtainable Brainrots in-game of a single Mutation will unlock a different, unique variant, or skin, for your base which are interchangeable by clicking the settings icon and scrolling down and choosing an unlocked base skin from the drop down menu.

You can see how much Mutation you have for the topic discussed above in the index. The most common mutations are Gold, with Diamond following up.

Mutation have the same multiplier value, being 10x.

The Best Mutation as of now will be Celestial .

SpyderSammy, Also Known As Just "Sammy" is a famous ROBLOX game developer, best known for being the Chief Production Officer of Do Big Studios™.




Overview

Aside from Sammy's contributions on Steal a Brainrot (Game), he has also helped on other well-known experiences, which include Blade Ball, Race Clicker, Schedule, and Fisch to name a few. He has also served as a tester for other communities in the Roblox space.

History

SpyderSammy's history in game development started in 2013, having developer roles in several known experiences. His first original project was created in 2022 and was called Crab Game, created and derived from a creation on the Steam platform called Crab Game, developed by Daniel Sooman (DaniDev). This project was handled under the Spyder Games group on the Roblox platform.

After the creation of Crab Game, SpyderSammy would soon develop Mining Clicker Simulator. This game was created and handled under the Spyder Crew group on the Roblox platform and had other developers that SpyderSammy worked in hand before the game's creation.

Before the start of another game creation, SpyderSammy would join the development team behind Blade Ball as a lead developer in late 2023. The details of what encompassed his responsibilities are unknown.

In 2024, SpyderSammy would release Pixel Tower Defense, a game majorly developed by the Blade Ball developers. This game was created and handled under the Red Spyder group on the Roblox platform. The game itself is no longer playable and was discontinued in April 2025 after accruing 26M+ place visits.

In the beginning of 2025, SpyderSammy would release Line to Riches, a game created to help players gather donations. This game has gained 15.6M+ place visits over its lifetime and is still accessible as of March 2026. This game was created and handled under the Blue Spyder group on the Roblox platform. Later in the year, SpyderSammy would release and co-found the group BRAZILIAN SPYDER along with Luke Luke Noom, this group would release the game Steal a Brainrot.

Rebirth
SIGN IN TO SAVE

Rebirth is a mechanic that, when done, takes the required brainrots and most of your money, but gives the ability to purchase new gear in the Coins Shop, gives new multipliers and modifies the base by adding new slots or new controllers. It requires a specific amount of cash and certain brainrot(s).

As the player gets more rebirths, the requirements begin to include higher income and rarer brainrots, along with more cash as a bonus when rebirthing.

Rebirth is an very important feature as it gives you perks like extra slots and money multiplier.

ADVERTISEMENT
Strategies
You can collect the next rebirth(s)' requirements and keep them on an alternate account or give it to a trusted person and get it back whenever you want. This will save your time.
It's smart to look at this page then collect all of the required Brainrots all at once, so you can rebirth faster! (Store them on an alt account, using your phone or a laptop/computer.)

It was reverted in Rebirth 18 as the requirement got buffed to only needing 1 brainrot.
Rebirth 19 is the first Rebirth to require a brainrot above $10M/s.
Rebirth 17 is first rebirth to require 2 ritual brainrots.

Overview

The Base is where the Brainrots you buy/steal are stored. The Brainrots inside your base will periodically generate cash for you, like how Base is worked in a tycoon game. There are skins for the Base, each requires to fill the index to obtain. The skin of your Base can be changed at any time in the game settings. Sammy once announced that you will soon be able to move your base to empty base spaces on the map.

Starting in Update 53, you can trade Seasonal Base.

Appearance

The Base is a building that can currently only have up to 3 floors, depending on how much rebirths you have. Sammy once said a basement was coming soon.

Floors	Slots	Rebirths

	Unknown

The Base has a lock of 60 seconds. You can add +10 seconds base lock per rebirth, as well as a fixed +10 seconds with the VIP gamepass.

Skins

In the game, there are currently in total 26 base skins - 4 Classics, 7 from Limited-Time events, 8 from Mutations and 5 OG Bases. 25 including Admin-Exclusive bases.

Mutation Base Skins can be obtainable at any time even outside the respective Mutation Season, but is untradeable. Initially Mutation Base Skins are supposed to be unobtainable, but is still obtainable due to bug. When the bug was patched in Update 52.8, community are angry, so the decision was reverted in the announcement.

Starting in Update 53, you can obtain all the Past Mutation Base Skins. You can view the Mutation's respective index to view the Brainrots that are needed to unlock Mutation Base Skins and the least recent the mutation is, the least Brainrots you need to index to unlock the specific Mutation Base Skin.
